Crystal Magic, also known as **Crystalline Sorcery**, harnesses the unique properties of crystals to amplify and direct magical energy. Crystals serve as conduits, as catalysts, and, in some instances, as containers for magic. They are the bridge between the ethereal realm of sorcery and the tangible world.

In their natural state, crystals vibrate with their own innate energy. But, when attuned by a skilled sorcerer, they resonate with the magic woven into them, amplifying the potency and range of the spell. As such, the key to understanding Crystal Magic lies in understanding the resonance of crystals themselves.
