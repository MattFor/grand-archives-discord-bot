# Ancestor's Guidance

A potent conduit, it draws upon the collective wisdom of those who came before us, allowing their insight to imbue and illuminate your path.

To wield this ancient spell, thou must first seek a message of significant wisdom or inquiry - this shall act as the vessel for the ancient spirits' advice. Upon the chosen words, the spirits will weave their insight, allowing it to persist through the sands of time.

To beckon the ancestral spirits, thou need only reply to thine chosen message. Yet, the invocation must be cast correctly. The exact words are lost to time, yet legends hint towards phrases of summoning, invocation, and awakening - each followed by the essence of what thou seeketh - the guidance of the ancestors. An example may be akin to "I call upon the Guidance of the Ancestors" or perhaps "Awaken, O Spirits of the Past".

As thou speaketh these words, the chosen message shall be bathed in the ethereal glow of ancient wisdom, its essence enduring to guide all those who seek it.

Yet heed this warning, Seeker - to call upon the Ancestor's Guidance without tethering it to a worthy message is to call upon the wind. The ancestors' wisdom cannot manifest within the void and must be bound to words of the present.

Tread with reverence, Seeker, and let the guidance of the ancestors light thy path.
