**Learning Crystal Magic**

To learn Crystal Magic, one must first learn the language of crystals. Each crystal has its own "voice", a distinct vibrational signature. The sage must listen to these vibrations, learn to interpret them, and eventually, manipulate them.

Start with simpler crystals, like quartz. Meditate with the crystal in your hand, feel its energy, and attune your mind to its vibrations. Over time, you will begin to sense a deeper connection, a resonance that goes beyond the physical.
