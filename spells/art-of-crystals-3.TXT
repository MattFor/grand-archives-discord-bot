Practicing Crystal Magic is an exercise in patience and precision. The sorcerer must weave their spell and then channel it into the crystal, harmonizing their magic with the crystal's own energy.

Begin with a basic light spell. Hold the crystal in your left hand and extend your right hand above it. Visualize the spell in your mind, then channel your magic into the crystal. If done correctly, the crystal will begin to glow, its light amplified by your magic.

As you become more adept, you can experiment with more complex spells and different types of crystals. Remember, the key to mastering Crystal Magic lies in understanding the crystals themselves. Each one is a new voice, a new song in the grand symphony of sorcery.

The most powerful of amplifiers are crystal staffs as well as crystal balls, the higher the density, concentration of the material. The wider the array of crystals formed inside of the cast spell.
